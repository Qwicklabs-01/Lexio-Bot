What is AI Watermark Removal?

Watermark removal is the process of intelligently deleting semi-transparent or solid overlays from images. While traditional methods involve complex cloning in Photoshop, our **AI Watermark Remover** automates this by predicting textures and patterns. It is ideal for cleaning up family photos, removing accidental date stamps, or prepping images for professional presentations.
