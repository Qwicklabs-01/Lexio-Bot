Modern UI Style Generation

High-end digital design often relies on subtle layer effects to create depth, realism, and visual hierarchy. Our **Style Design Generator** brings the power of professional design software like Photoshop and Figma to your browser. Whether you are aiming for the sleek, frosted look of Glassmorphism or the soft tactile feel of Neumorphism, this tool generates the corresponding CSS `box-shadow` and `background` properties instantly.

Understanding Design Effects

Every professional effect is built from fundamental components:

  • Drop Shadows: Creating separation between the element and the background.
  • Bevels & Emboss: Simulated by using light and dark shadows on opposite sides.
  • Transparency: Essential for modern "Glass" effects that blur the underlying background.
