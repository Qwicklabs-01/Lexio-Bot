What is an Optical Character Recognition (OCR)?

Optical Character Recognition (OCR) is a technology that recognizes text within a digital image. It is commonly used to recognize text in scanned documents and images. Our OCR tool uses the powerful Tesseract.js engine to perform high-accuracy text extraction directly in your browser. This means your images are never uploaded to a server, ensuring 100% privacy and security.

How to Use the OCR Tool

  1. Upload your image: Drag and drop your file or click the upload area to select a document from your device.
  2. Wait for processing: Our AI engine will analyze the image. The progress bar shows you the real-time status of the recognition.
  3. Edit and Copy: Once finished, the text will appear in the box below. You can edit it manually or click "Copy Text" to use it elsewhere.

Why Choose Universal Studio OCR?

Unlike many other online OCR services, Universal Studio works **on the client side**. This means your sensitive documents never leave your computer. It is perfect for scanning receipts, contracts, or handwritten notes while maintaining total control over your data. Plus, it's completely free and optimized for mobile devices.
