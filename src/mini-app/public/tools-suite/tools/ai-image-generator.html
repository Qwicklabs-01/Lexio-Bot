Best Practices for Prompting

To get the highest quality results, follow these prompting guidelines:

  • Be Descriptive: Instead of "a cat," try "a regal Siamese cat sitting on a velvet throne, cinematic lighting."
  • Define the Style: Mention specific artistic styles like "Cyberpunk," "Renaissance," or "Minimalist."
  • Lighting & Mood: Use words like "golden hour," "moody," "vibrant," or "soft focus" to set the atmosphere.
