Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

1. Our Commitment to Privacy

At Universal Studio, your privacy is our top priority. We believe that you should have access to powerful tools without sacrificing your personal data. That's why we've designed our platform to be privacy-first.

2. No Data Uploads

Unlike traditional online tools, Universal Studio processes your files (images, PDFs, documents) directly on your device. We use modern Web APIs and client-side processing to ensure that your sensitive data never leaves your computer or smartphone.

3. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information, and we do not store any of the content you process using our tools. We may collect anonymous usage statistics (such as which tools are most popular) to improve our service, but this data can never be linked to an individual user.

4. Third-Party Services

We use Google AdSense to serve advertisements. AdSense may use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to our website or other websites on the Internet.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our contact page.
